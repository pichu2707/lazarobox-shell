## Known issues found by these records

These are lazarobox issues to fix, not terminal fails, except where a row above says `fail`.

- Notice truncated at narrow widths: at ~55 columns the OSC 52 notice is cut to `Sent to the terminal` and the hint is lost.
- Focus reports (`ESC[I`/`ESC[O`) are not forwarded to panes.
- Model-marks: engine rows in a popup narrower than 62 columns lose the engine name and the codex badge; with `TERM=linux` claude shows `to…`.
- Click was not separately checked (pending); the copy over SSH, PATH-stripped and paste-scenario rows are pending.
